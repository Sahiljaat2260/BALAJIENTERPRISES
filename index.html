What We Do

Our Services

We provide comprehensive telecom services across infrastructure build and complete Operation & Maintenance — handling everything from the first foundation pour to keeping sites live 24×7. One trusted vendor, full site lifecycle.

🏗️   Infrastructure Works
🏗️

Foundation Laying

Complete civil foundation work for telecom towers — site survey, excavation, rebar placement, concrete pouring and curing as per structural drawings.

📡

BTS Installation & Commissioning

End-to-end installation of Base Transceiver Stations — equipment mounting, antenna alignment, RF cabling, feeder runs and site commissioning.

🏠

Shelter & Infrastructure Erection

Pre-fabricated shelter installation, BTS cabin erection, fence & boundary works, earthing and lightning protection installation.

🔌

Cable Laying

Structured cable laying for power and RF — trenching, conduit laying, feeder cable runs, earthing cables and inter-equipment cabling with proper termination.

⚡

Power & Electrical Works

EB power connections, earthing systems, indoor/outdoor power cabling and commissioning of power plants and rectifiers at telecom sites.

🚗

Vehicle & Fleet Management

Dedicated vehicle deployment and fleet coordination for field teams — tracked and documented across all project sites and states.

🔧   Operation & Maintenance (O&M)
❄️

AC Servicing & Repair

Preventive and corrective maintenance of shelter air-conditioning units — cleaning, gas charging, compressor checks and fault rectification to maintain thermal SLAs.

🔋

DG (Diesel Generator) Servicing

Scheduled servicing, oil changes, filter replacement, load testing and breakdown repair of DG sets to ensure maximum uptime at all tower sites.

⛽

Diesel Vending & Management

Timely diesel supply to remote and urban tower sites with quantity tracking, receipts and consumption reporting for full auditability.

🛠️

Preventive Maintenance (PM)

Scheduled PM visits covering passive infra inspection, battery health checks, shelter cleaning, earthing verification and alarm clearance.

🚨

Breakdown & Corrective Maintenance

Rapid field response to site alarms and equipment failures — fault isolation, component repair and site restoration within operator SLA timelines.

📋

Complete Passive O&M Management

Full turnkey passive O&M of telecom tower sites — security, power management, uptime tracking, MIS reporting and end-to-end documentation.
